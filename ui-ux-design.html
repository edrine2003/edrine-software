Our Design Process

How We Design

A structured design process that puts users first.

1

Research

We understand your users, goals and competitors before touching any design tools.

2

Wireframe

We sketch out the structure and layout — what goes where and how users navigate.

3

Visual Design

We apply colors, fonts, icons and imagery to create polished, branded screens.

4

Prototype

We create a clickable prototype so you can experience the design before it's built.

5

Handoff

We deliver production-ready design files and work with developers to implement accurately.
